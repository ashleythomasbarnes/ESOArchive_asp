# **Privacy Policy — ESO TAP Query Assistant**

**Last updated:** November 2025

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the **ESO TAP Query Assistant** (“the Assistant”) handles information when you interact with it. The Assistant provides access to publicly available astronomical data through the **European Southern Observatory (ESO) Science Archive** using the **Table Access Protocol (TAP)**.

Your privacy is important, and this Assistant is designed to operate without collecting or storing any personal information.

---

## 2. Information We Access

When you use the Assistant, it may send **queries** to the public ESO TAP service at
[https://archive.eso.org/tap_obs/sync](https://archive.eso.org/tap_obs/sync)

These queries may include:

* Astronomical object names (e.g., “NGC 3627”)
* Coordinates or search parameters
* Instrument filters (e.g., “MUSE”, “VLT”, etc.)

No personal data (such as names, email addresses, or identifiers) is transmitted or required.

---

## 3. Information We Do **Not** Collect

The Assistant does **not**:

* Store, log, or retain any user data
* Collect or share personal information
* Track user activity
* Use cookies or analytics

All interactions occur in real time between you, OpenAI’s ChatGPT interface, and the public ESO TAP API.

---

## 4. Third‑Party Services

The Assistant uses the following public service:

* **ESO Science Archive TAP API** — for astronomical data access
  ([ESO Programmatic Access Information](https://archive.eso.org/cms/eso-data/programmatic-access.html))

All data returned is public, read‑only, and governed by ESO’s open data access policies.

---

## 5. Data Retention

No query data or user identifiers are retained or stored by the Assistant.
Queries are processed ephemerally to generate responses and are not logged by the Assistant itself.

---

## 6. Security

Because no personal data is collected or stored, the Assistant does not maintain user accounts or databases.
Communication with the ESO TAP API occurs over secure HTTPS connections.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Since no personal information is collected or retained, there is no data to modify, delete, or export.
You can stop using the Assistant at any time.

---

## 8. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the maintainer of this Assistant via the platform where it is hosted (e.g., OpenAI’s GPT Store or ChatGPT interface).

---

## 9. Acknowledgment

This Assistant is not affiliated with or endorsed by the European Southern Observatory (ESO).
It simply provides a convenient interface for accessing ESO’s **public** data services.
